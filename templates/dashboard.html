Metric & Button Explanations

  • Thaals Out/In: The total count of thaals that crossed the 'Out' (Kitchen to Hall) and 'In' (Hall to Kitchen) lines for the selected day.
  • Expected Thaals: The optional number entered by the manager at the start of the service.
  • Service Duration: The total combined time in minutes for all service sessions on the selected day.
  • Out Duration: The time in minutes from the first thaal going out to the last thaal going out. Measures the length of the serving period.
  • In Duration: The time in minutes from the first thaal coming in to the last thaal coming in. Measures the length of the return/cleanup period.
  • Full Cycle: The time in minutes from the very first thaal going out to the very last thaal coming back in. Measures the entire operational window.
  • Discrepancy Rate: A measure of counting accuracy. It shows the percentage of thaals that went out but were not counted coming back in for the selected day.
  • Export Day's Event Log: Downloads a detailed CSV file with a timestamp for every single thaal that was counted in or out on the selected day.
  • Export All Sessions: Downloads a summary CSV file containing one row for every service session ever recorded, with start/end times and final counts.
